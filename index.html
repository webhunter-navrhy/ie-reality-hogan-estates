Hogan Estates are delighted to present No. 3 Annesley Bridge Road to the market. This spacious and well-proportioned terraced residence extends to approximately 121 sq.m. (1,302 sq.ft.), offering generous accommodation in one of Dublin's most convenient and well-established residential locations.

The property is a late Edwardian property with a lot of the original features intact.

The property benefits from electric heating provided by various individual units and uPVC double glazed windows, providing comfortable and low-maintenance living accommodation. Ideally suited to a wide range of purchasers including first-time buyers, growing families and investors alike, this home presents an excellent opportunity to acquire a substantial property close to Dublin City Centre.

Amenities

Annesley Bridge Road enjoys an enviable location in the heart of Fairview, with an exceptional range of amenities within easy walking distance. Fairview Park is nearby and offers extensive recreational facilities including playing pitches, walking and cycling paths, tennis courts and children's playgrounds. Sporting enthusiasts are also well catered for with Clontarf Golf Club, Westwood Club Clontarf, Clontarf Rugby Club, Clontarf Cricket Club and numerous GAA and football clubs all within close proximity.

The area boasts a vibrant cultural and recreational scene, with the historic villages of Fairview and Clontarf offering an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants and pubs. The nearby 3Arena, Bord Gáis Energy Theatre, Croke Park and Dublin City Centre provide an abundance of entertainment, sporting and cultural attractions throughout the year. The magnificent Clontarf Seafront promenade and St. Anne's Park are also just a short distance away, offering beautiful outdoor spaces for walking, cycling and leisure.

The Area

The area is exceptionally well served by retail amenities, with Fairview providing an excellent selection of local shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday services. Clontarf, Drumcondra and Dublin City Centre are all within easy reach, while Fairview Strand, East Point Business Park and the IFSC are only a short distance away, making this an ideal location for those working in the city.

Education

Families will appreciate the excellent choice of educational facilities nearby, including St. Joseph's CBS, St. Mary's National School, Scoil Mhuire Marino, St. Vincent's Secondary School, Marino College and Holy Faith Secondary School. Third-level institutions including Dublin City University (DCU), Trinity College Dublin and Technological University Dublin are also easily accessible.

Transport

Transport links are superb, with numerous Dublin Bus routes serving the immediate area, providing frequent services to Dublin City Centre and surrounding suburbs. Clontarf Road DART Station is within easy reach, offering fast rail connections across Dublin, while Connolly Station is also nearby for national rail and LUAS Red Line services. The Port Tunnel, M50 motorway, Dublin Airport and all major national road networks are readily accessible, ensuring excellent connectivity for commuters and travellers alike.

No. 3 Annesley Bridge Road combines generous living space with an outstanding location close to every conceivable amenity, making it a superb opportunity for purchasers seeking a quality home in one of Dublin's most desirable city suburbs.
